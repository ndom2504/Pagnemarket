PagneMarket / Account deletion

Delete my PagneMarket account

PagneMarket app — operated by Export Monde Prestige Inc.

1. From the app (immediate)

  1. Open PagneMarket and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab, then Settings (Paramètres).
  3. In the danger zone, tap “Supprimer mon compte” (Delete my account).
  4. Confirm twice. Your account is deleted immediately.

2. Without access to the app

Send an email from the address (or mentioning the phone number) linked to your account to info@misterdil.ca with the subject “PagneMarket account deletion”. We process requests within 30 days.

3. Data deleted

  • Name, email, phone number, password and profile photo
  • Google and Apple sign-in links
  • Favorites, cart, notifications, blocks and push notification tokens
  • Public shop or creator profile and listings (removed from the platform)

4. Data retained

Order and transaction history is kept in anonymized form (no name or contact details) for as long as required by accounting and tax obligations, up to 7 years, then deleted. Messages sent to other users remain visible to them under the name “Deleted account”.

See also our privacy policy.
